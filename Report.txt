### How to Present the Content Safely
You asked whether you should completely remove words or censor them with asterisks (e.g., "m**der" or "r*pe"). 

**Do not rely on asterisk censorship.** It is a highly ineffective strategy for two reasons:
1. **Algorithmic Failure:** Modern moderation tools (like Google's Perspective API or Vercel's automated Web Application Firewalls) do not read text like old-school keyword filters. They use transformer-based Natural Language Processing (NLP) that analyzes the surrounding context and sub-word tokens. If a user writes "I will k*ll you," the AI mathematically predicts the masked word and flags the entire sentence for `SEVERE_TOXICITY` anyway. Asterisks will not hide your database from Vercel or Supabase scanners.
2. **Psychological Failure:** From a user experience standpoint, reading "r*pe" triggers the exact same cognitive and emotional response in a human reader as the uncensored word.

Since your platform's core value relies on the authenticity of the "worst things said," completely removing the words ruins the premise of the site. Instead, you should present the raw text but use **Structural Encapsulation**:

1. **UI Interstitials / Content Warnings:** Instead of censoring individual words, hide the entire textual quote behind a blurred screen or a clickable overlay that says: *"Content Warning: This post contains explicit descriptions of violence/abuse.
